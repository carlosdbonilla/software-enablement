# 00 — Elastic Learning Path: Senior Account Executive, DoD / Defense Agencies

> Built for: **Senior AE, DoD / Defense Agencies** at **Endgame Systems, LLC** (Elastic's government subsidiary)
> Sources: the role posting, Elastic's *Elasticsearch Engineer 8.1* training course, and Elastic public-sector materials (see [Sources](#sources))

---

## The job in one sentence

**Sell Elastic's search, security and observability platform to DoD Defense Agencies (DISA and the 4th Estate), usually through system integrators and contract vehicles, by tying the technology to mission outcomes.**

## What you need to be able to do by the end

1. **Explain Elastic** in 30 seconds, 2 minutes and 10 minutes
2. **Understand the technology** well enough to have a credible conversation and know when to bring in the Solutions Architect
3. **Speak DoD**: its structure, networks, priorities and acronyms
4. **Know how DoD buys**: fiscal year, vehicles, SIs, ATOs
5. **Run discovery and qualify a deal** with MEDDPICC
6. **Handle competitors and objections**
7. **Interview well**: your story, a territory plan, and sharp questions

## How to study (so it sticks)

Every lesson has the same shape:

1. **🎯 Goal** — what you're learning
2. **💡 The Idea** — plain English + an analogy
3. **🔑 Key Terms** — the words you must know
4. **🗣️ Say It** — practice **out loud** (sales is a spoken skill)
5. **⚠️ Watch Out** — common mistakes
6. **🧠 Recall** — answer *without looking*, then check

**Three habits**
- **Say every 🗣️ Say It out loud.** Record yourself on your phone once per lesson and play it back.
- **Answer Recall questions before opening the answers.** Struggling to remember is what builds memory.
- **Connect everything to a mission.** In DoD, "faster search" means nothing. "An analyst finds the intruder in minutes instead of days" means everything.
