## 🎯 Goal
Walk into every interview round with your story, a territory plan, strong answers and sharp questions.

## 💡 The Idea
An AE interview is a **sales call where you're the product**. Use what you've learned: do discovery on the role, qualify the opportunity, handle objections (your gaps), and **ask for the next step**.

## Typical interview stages

| Stage | They're testing | Prepare |
|---|---|---|
| **Recruiter screen** | Fit, motivation, comp, clearance | 60-second story, why Elastic, why DoD |
| **Hiring manager** | Sales skill, territory knowledge, coachability | Deal stories (STAR), MEDDPICC, DoD fluency |
| **Peer / SA / partner panel** | Collaboration, technical credibility | Lessons 03–04, how you work with an SA and SIs |
| **Final / presentation** | Strategic thinking, executive presence | **Territory plan** or mock discovery (below) |

## 1. Your story (Present → Past → Future, 60–90 seconds)

Fill in the brackets with your real details:

> **Present:** "I'm at IBM, selling and prospecting for **Maximo**, IBM's enterprise asset management platform, into [industries / account types]. I build pipeline from scratch and translate technical capabilities into operational outcomes."
>
> **Past:** "Before that I worked as a **solutions engineer**, so I'm comfortable going deep with technical teams and know how to partner with an SA. I'm finishing an **MBA in Business Analytics & Strategy at UF** and hold **IBM Cloud & AI** and **Databricks** certifications, so data platforms and AI are where I've been building depth."
>
> **Future:** "I want to sell a platform that's mission-critical, in a market where getting it right matters. Elastic's position in DoD cyber defense, plus its ability to run anywhere, including air-gapped, makes this the role I want next."

**Connect Maximo to Elastic:** both are about **operational data for mission readiness**. Maximo tracks assets and maintenance; Elastic searches and secures the data those systems generate. If you've touched any public-sector or defense-related Maximo accounts, **lead with them**.

## 2. Address the gap head-on

The posting asks for **3+ years selling SaaS to DoD/IC**. Don't wait for them to raise it:

> "I'll be direct: I haven't carried a DoD quota yet. Here's how I'd close that gap quickly. I've already studied the 4th Estate, DISA's role, how DoD buys through vehicles and SIs, and Elastic's defense proof points. In my first 90 days I'd lean on the SA and partner teams and the existing customer base to learn the accounts. What I bring is [pipeline building, enterprise selling, technical depth]. Is there anything in my background that concerns you for this territory?"

That last question **surfaces the objection** so you can handle it in the room.

**Clearance:** state plainly whether you hold one, held one, or are willing and able to get one.

## 3. Why Elastic? Why DoD? (memorize 3 reasons each)

**Why Elastic**
1. One platform across security, observability and AI → land and expand
2. Proven in DoD at scale (Air Force 600K+ endpoints, CISA partnership)
3. Runs anywhere, including air-gapped, so it fits classified and edge missions others can't serve

**Why DoD / Defense Agencies**
1. Mission impact: cyber defense and decision speed matter for national security
2. Huge demand drivers: Zero Trust by FY27, CJADC2, AI adoption, modernization
3. Long-term relationships and large enterprise deals

## 4. Likely questions & how to answer

Use **STAR** for experience questions: **Situation → Task → Action → Result** (with a number).

| Question | What a strong answer includes |
|---|---|
| "Walk me through a deal you're proud of." | STAR; your role in discovery, champion, competition, paper process; a $ or % result |
| "How do you build pipeline in a new territory?" | Account research → map org/funding → SI and partner alignment → multi-threaded outreach → events → track ratios |
| "How do you qualify a deal?" | MEDDPICC with a DoD example ([Lesson 09](09-qualifying-meddpicc.md)) |
| "Tell me about a deal you lost." | Honest; what you learned; what you'd do differently (usually champion or economic buyer gaps) |
| "How would you work with system integrators?" | Map which SIs hold programs in your accounts; give them win themes; joint account plans; make them look good |
| "What do you know about DISA?" | DoD's IT backbone (networks, enterprise services, DODIN defense); DoD-wide scale ([Lesson 05](05-dod-101.md)) |
| "How does federal fiscal timing affect your forecast?" | FY Oct–Sep, Q4 rush, CR risk, colors of money ([Lesson 07](07-how-dod-buys.md)) |
| "Pitch Elastic to a CISO in 2 minutes." | Mission problem → Elastic → proof → ask ([Lessons 02](02-elastic-in-one-page.md), [06](06-elastic-for-defense.md)) |
| "A customer says, 'We're a Splunk shop.' What do you do?" | Acknowledge → Ask → Answer → Advance ([Lesson 10](10-competition-and-objections.md)) |
| "How do you work with an SA?" | Bring them in after discovery; define roles before calls; you own the deal strategy, they own the technical win |

## 5. Territory plan (for the final round)

Keep it to **5–7 slides**:

| Slide | Content |
|---|---|
| **1. Territory snapshot** | Key agencies (DISA, DLA, DHA, DTRA, DCSA…), their missions, known Elastic footprint (ask for this) |
| **2. Market drivers** | Zero Trust FY27, CJADC2, cyber threats, AI, consolidation, JWCC |
| **3. Account prioritization** | Tier 1/2/3 by budget, pain, incumbent renewals and existing relationships |
| **4. Plays** | e.g., "SIEM data-retention play vs. per-GB incumbents," "Endpoint modernization," "Trusted AI on mission data" |
| **5. Partner strategy** | Which SIs and resellers, on which programs and vehicles |
| **6. 30-60-90** | See below |
| **7. Metrics** | Pipeline targets, meetings, partner-sourced pipeline, first closes |

### 30-60-90 days

| Phase | Focus | Outcomes |
|---|---|---|
| **Days 1–30: Learn** | Product training, account reviews, meet SAs/partners/leadership, shadow calls, learn vehicles | Account map for every agency; know the installed base |
| **Days 31–60: Engage** | Meet existing customers, align with 2–3 key SIs, start outreach to priority accounts | First qualified opportunities; partner account plans |
| **Days 61–90: Build** | Run discovery and pilots with SAs, align to FY27 budget timing | Pipeline at target (e.g., 3× quota building), first forecastable deals |

## 6. Questions to ask (pick 3–4 per round)

**Hiring manager**
1. Is this a new territory or an existing book? Which agencies are in scope, and are IC agencies included?
2. Which use case usually opens the door in defense: Security, Observability or Search & AI?
3. How much business comes through SIs and vehicles vs. direct?
4. What separates your top-performing AEs?
5. What does success look like at 90 days and at one year? How long is a typical ramp?

**Peers / SAs**
6. How do AEs and SAs split work on a typical deal?
7. Which competitor do you face most in DoD, and how do you win?

**Leadership**
8. How are the CISA partnership and Elastic's AI features shaping DoD conversations?
9. How does clearance status affect day-one effectiveness? Does Endgame sponsor clearances?

**Always close with:**
> "Based on our conversation, is there anything that would keep you from moving me to the next round?"

## 🗣️ Say It
1. Record your **60-second story**. Listen back. Cut anything that doesn't support "why me for this role."
2. Answer **"Walk me through a deal you're proud of"** with STAR in 2 minutes.
3. Deliver the **gap answer** out loud until it sounds confident, not defensive.

## ⚠️ Watch Out
- **Don't overclaim DoD experience.** Interviewers in this space spot it immediately.
- **Use numbers** in every deal story (% of quota, deal size, cycle time).
- **Send a thank-you note** within 24 hours, referencing one specific thing you discussed.
- **Comp:** the posted OTE is $226.5K–$340K. If asked, give a range inside it that reflects your experience.
