## 🎯 Goal
Understand how DoD is organized, where your territory fits, and what it cares about right now.

## 💡 The Idea
The **Department of Defense (DoD)** is the largest organization in the U.S. Your territory isn't the Army or Navy. It's the **agencies that support all of them**.

> **Analogy:** If the military services are the **teams on the field**, the Defense Agencies are the **league's shared services**: the network, the supply chain, the hospitals, the intelligence. Everyone depends on them.

> ℹ️ Since a September 2025 executive order, "**Department of War**" is used as a secondary name for DoD. You'll see both. Follow the customer's lead.

## How DoD is organized

```
                        Secretary of Defense (OSD)
                                  │
     ┌────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────┐
 MILITARY DEPARTMENTS      COMBATANT COMMANDS          "4th ESTATE"  ← YOUR TERRITORY
 Army · Navy (+ Marines)   CYBERCOM, INDOPACOM,     Defense Agencies & DoD Field
 Air Force (+ Space Force) EUCOM, SOCOM, etc.       Activities: DISA, DLA, DHA, DTRA…
 (organize, train, equip)  (fight the wars)         (shared support for everyone)
```

**4th Estate** = the DoD organizations that are **not** a military department or combatant command. That's about two dozen agencies and field activities.

## Key Defense Agencies to know

| Agency | Mission (one line) | Why Elastic fits |
|---|---|---|
| **DISA** — Defense Information Systems Agency | Runs DoD's IT backbone: networks, cloud, enterprise services, cyber defense of the network (DODIN) | Huge security and network data; enterprise services; cloud contracts |
| **DLA** — Defense Logistics Agency | DoD's supply chain: fuel, food, parts | Logistics app monitoring, search across supply data |
| **DHA** — Defense Health Agency | Military health system | Security + compliance for health data; app uptime |
| **DTRA** — Defense Threat Reduction Agency | Counters weapons of mass destruction | Data fusion, search across intelligence/technical data |
| **DCSA** — Defense Counterintelligence & Security Agency | Security clearances, insider threat | Investigation search, insider-threat analytics |
| **DCMA / DCAA** | Contract management / audit | Document search, analytics |
| **MDA** — Missile Defense Agency | Missile defense programs | Observability for mission systems, security |
| **DIA / NSA / NGA** | Intelligence combat support agencies (also part of the IC) | Often a separate IC team — confirm whether they're in scope |

> 🎯 **DISA is the anchor.** It runs DoD-wide networks and services, so a win there can scale across the department.

## DoD networks (know these cold)

| Network | Classification |
|---|---|
| **NIPRNet** ("nipper") | Unclassified |
| **SIPRNet** ("sipper") | Secret |
| **JWICS** ("jay-wicks") | Top Secret / SCI |
| **DIL / tactical edge** | Disconnected, intermittent, limited bandwidth: ships, forward bases, aircraft |

**Why this matters for you:** many tools only run in a commercial cloud. Elastic runs **self-managed and air-gapped**, so the **same platform** can run on NIPR, SIPR, JWICS and at the edge.

## What DoD cares about now (connect every pitch to these)

| Priority | What it means | Elastic angle |
|---|---|---|
| **Zero Trust** | "Never trust, always verify." The DoD Zero Trust Strategy targets implementation by **FY2027** across 7 pillars (user, device, network, application, data, **visibility & analytics**, automation) | Visibility & analytics + automation pillars = SIEM; device pillar = endpoint |
| **CJADC2** (Combined Joint All-Domain Command & Control) | Connect sensors and shooters across all services and domains, so decisions happen faster | Data fusion and search across siloed, multi-format data |
| **Cyber defense** | Nation-state threats; defending the network (DODIN) | SIEM + endpoint at massive scale |
| **Cloud** | **JWCC** (Joint Warfighting Cloud Capability) contract with AWS, Google, Microsoft and Oracle | Elastic runs on all four clouds and on-prem |
| **AI adoption** | Move AI from pilots to trusted production | RAG/agents on agency data, with approved models |
| **Efficiency & speed** | Pressure to cut duplicate tools and buy commercial software faster | Consolidation onto one platform |

## 🗣️ Say It
Explain to a friend in 60 seconds: *"What's the 4th Estate, why is DISA important, and why does 'runs air-gapped' matter in DoD?"*

## ⚠️ Watch Out
- Get the acronyms right. Mispronouncing or misusing them signals "outsider" fast.
- Don't assume IC agencies (NSA, NGA, DIA) are in your territory. **Ask in the interview.**
- Lead with **mission**, not technology: "readiness," "decision advantage," "defending the network."
