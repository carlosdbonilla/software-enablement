# 09 — Qualifying Deals with MEDDPICC

---

## 🎯 Goal
Tell a **real** deal from a "happy ears" deal, and know what's missing.

## 💡 The Idea
**MEDDPICC** is a checklist that enterprise sales teams use to qualify deals. Each letter is something you must know before you forecast a deal.

> **Analogy:** MEDDPICC is a **pre-flight checklist**. A plane can look ready, but if one item isn't checked, you don't take off. Forecasting a deal with gaps is taking off without fuel.

> ℹ️ Ask in the interview which framework Elastic uses. Many enterprise software companies use MEDDPICC or a variant.

## MEDDPICC — with the DoD angle

| Letter | Means | Key question | In DoD, sounds like… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **M** | **Metrics** | What measurable outcome do they need? | Mean time to detect/respond, endpoints covered, days of data retained, tools retired, analyst hours saved |
| **E** | **Economic buyer** | Who controls the money and can say yes? | Program manager, CIO or CISO with **funded** authority |
| **D** | **Decision criteria** | How will they judge options? | ATO/IL compliance, air-gapped support, scale, cost, integration |
| **D** | **Decision process** | What steps lead to a decision? | Evaluation → pilot → security review → ATO → acquisition approval |
| **P** | **Paper process** | How does the contract get signed? | Vehicle + reseller/SI + contracting officer + **FY deadline** |
| **I** | **Identify pain** | What hurts enough to act? | Breach, audit finding, expiring contract, mandate (Zero Trust FY27), mission failure |
| **C** | **Champion** | Who sells for you inside? | SOC lead, engineer, SI architect, mission owner who **wins if Elastic wins** |
| **C** | **Competition** | Who else is in it? | Splunk, Microsoft Sentinel, CrowdStrike, Trellix, OpenSearch, **status quo** |

**Memory trick:** **M**y **E**ager **D**og **D**igs **P**its **I**n **C**ity **C**ourtyards.

## A qualified DoD deal has all 5 of these

1. ✅ **Real pain** tied to the mission
2. ✅ **Identified funding** and an economic buyer
3. ✅ **A known path to contract**: vehicle + partner
4. ✅ **A champion** with access to the economic buyer
5. ✅ **A compelling event**: renewal, mandate or FY deadline

## A test of a real champion
A real champion:
- **Has influence** and **access** to the economic buyer
- **Sells for you** when you're not in the room
- **Gives you inside information** (budget, competitors, politics)
- **Wins personally** if Elastic wins

A "friendly contact" just likes you. **Test them:** ask them to set up a meeting with the economic buyer.

## 🚩 Red flags and disqualifiers

- No funding or program office behind it
- No compelling event ("we're just exploring")
- Can't reach anyone with budget authority
- No contract vehicle or partner path
- Requirements written around a competitor's product (a "wired" deal)
- Champion can't explain why Elastic would win
- Environment needs Elastic can't meet (**check with your SA first**)

> 💡 **Disqualifying is a skill.** Time is your scarcest resource. Walking away from a bad deal early frees time for good ones.

## Example scoring

| | Deal A: DISA SOC | Deal B: "Interested engineer" |
|---|---|---|
| Metrics | Cut investigation time from days to hours; retain 12 months | ❓ |
| Economic buyer | CISO, met | ❌ unknown |
| Decision criteria | SIPR support, scale, cost | ❓ |
| Decision process | Pilot → ATO → buy in Q4 | ❌ |
| Paper | SEWP via reseller, SI involved | ❌ |
| Pain | Legacy SIEM renewal in 9 months, data dropped | "Looks cool" |
| Champion | SOC lead, tested | Friendly contact |
| Competition | Incumbent Splunk | ❓ |
| **Verdict** | **Qualified — forecast it** | **Nurture, don't forecast** |

## 🔑 Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Qualify / disqualify** | Decide whether a deal is real and worth time |
| **Forecast** | Committing to leadership when a deal will close |
| **Happy ears** | Hearing interest and mistaking it for a deal |
| **Wired deal** | Requirements written to favor a competitor |
| **Status quo** | Doing nothing, the most common "competitor" |

## 🗣️ Say It
Pick any deal from your IBM experience. Score it out loud against MEDDPICC. Which letters were weak? What would you have done differently?

## ⚠️ Watch Out
- Don't treat MEDDPICC as a form you fill in once. **Update it after every meeting.**
- **Status quo is the #1 competitor.** Pain + compelling event are how you beat it.

## 🧠 Recall
1. What does each letter of MEDDPICC stand for?
2. What 5 things make a DoD deal qualified?
3. How do you test whether someone is a real champion?
4. What is the most common "competitor"?

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Paper process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition.
2. Mission-tied pain, identified funding/economic buyer, a contract path (vehicle + partner), a champion with access, and a compelling event.
3. Ask them to do something that costs them, like setting up a meeting with the economic buyer, or ask for inside info.
4. Status quo (doing nothing).
</details>

## 📌 Remember
- **MEDDPICC = pre-flight checklist**
- **Pain + money + path + champion + deadline**
- **Disqualify early**, and update after every meeting
