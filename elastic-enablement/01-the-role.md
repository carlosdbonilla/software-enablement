## 🎯 Goal
Know exactly what this job is, who you'd work with, and how you'd be judged.

## 💡 The Idea

An **Account Executive (AE)** owns the revenue for a set of customers (a **territory**). You find the opportunities, run the deals, coordinate the team, and close. You carry a **quota**: a yearly revenue target.

> **Analogy:** The AE is the **quarterback**. You don't play every position, but you call the plays and you're accountable for the score.

### The team around you

| Who | What they do | When you bring them in |
|---|---|---|
| **Solutions Architect (SA)** | Technical expert: demos, architecture, pilots | Once there's a real problem to solve |
| **Partner / channel manager** | Manages relationships with system integrators and resellers | Early — most DoD deals go through partners |
| **Customer Success / Services** | Adoption and deployment after the sale | Before close, to plan delivery |
| **Sales leadership** | Forecast, deal strategy, executive help | Big deals, stuck deals |
| **Proposal / contracts team** | Federal paperwork and compliance | When there's an RFP or contract action |

### About the hiring company

**Endgame Systems, LLC** is a **wholly owned Elastic subsidiary** that serves government customers with heightened security needs.

- Elastic bought the endpoint security company **Endgame** in **2019**.
- Endgame's technology became **Elastic Defend** (Elastic's endpoint protection).
- That history gives Endgame credibility with defense and intelligence customers.

### What you'll do (from the posting)
- Own the sales strategy for Defense Agencies
- Build relationships with military and agency leaders
- Work with government stakeholders and **SIs** to design and deliver Elastic for joint and multi-domain operations
- Build proposals that fit federal acquisition rules
- Track mission priorities, funding trends and new technology
- Represent Elastic at defense events and briefings

### How you'll be judged

| Metric | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Quota attainment** | % of your revenue target closed |
| **Pipeline** | Total value of qualified opportunities (usually 3–4× quota) |
| **Forecast accuracy** | Do deals close when you said they would? |
| **Land & expand** | New customers won + growth of existing ones |
